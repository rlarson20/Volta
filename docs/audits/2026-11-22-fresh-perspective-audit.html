Where Your Guardrails Genuinely Worked

Solid Autograd Core

Your intuition is right to distinguish code quality from project momentum: the core Rust autograd engine is disciplined, clean, and safer than most hobby ML libraries.

  • Autograd invariants held: 100% edge() routing, zero borrow_mut() outside autograd.rs/ops//tensor.rs, iterative Drop preventing deep-DAG stack overflow, and zero unwrap() or unchecked indexing in library code.
  • Minimal, sound unsafe: only 4 unsafe sites in the entire crate (all GEMM calls in src/ops/matmul.rs), with explicit safety invariants and checked dimensions.
  • High-signal numerical engineering: PyTorch 2.15 fixture suite (tests/torch_ref_*.rs), pairwise summation (2^24 stall fix), K-blocked Accelerate SGEMM, atomic SafeTensors writes, and strict/atomic LoadPlan.

Where Claude Ran Away With the Project

Process > Shipping

Your unease is completely validated by the commit graph and GitHub metadata. Without strict scope boundaries, Claude built a self-sustaining governance, audit, and tooling apparatus around a frozen 0.3.0 crate.
